Why This Design Works

This is the same content as the bad version. Only the CSS changed. Use “Switch Design” to compare.

  1. One font family. Inter is used for every heading, paragraph and button.
  2. Limited palette. Navy, white and one blue accent. Blue always means “click me”.
  3. Clear hierarchy. The headline is biggest, then section titles, then body text.
  4. Consistent spacing. Every section has the same padding, and the cards sit on an equal 3-column grid.
  5. Readable text. High contrast and a line height of about 1.7.
  6. Consistent buttons. One shape, one color, one hover effect everywhere.
